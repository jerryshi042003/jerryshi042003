# Jerry Shi

Data engineer building reliable data products and visual explanations for sports.

## Done

- **[UCLA Tennis consulting](https://github.com/bruin-tennis-consulting/analytics)** — Turned four years of hand-tracked match coordinates into the [film-review app](https://match-viewing-dashboard.web.app/) used by UCLA Tennis, built with an 80-person student analyst team.
- **[Orfeu](https://github.com/jerryshi042003/orfeu)** — A [daily radio station](https://jerryshi042003.github.io/orfeu/) that drops into one of 6,000+ microgenres, then explains the scene behind the sound.

## In progress

- **[Tennis Motion Lab](https://github.com/jerryshi042003/tennis-motion-lab)** — Can one professional clip become a trustworthy free-camera lesson? Testing what monocular 3D preserves, where geometry fails, and how to label measured versus inferred motion. [Read the cited research](https://jerryshi042003.github.io/tennis-motion-lab/).
- **[Pro training drills](https://github.com/jerryshi042003/tennis-drill-corpus)** — A source-linked atlas of what Alcaraz, Sinner, Rune, and their teams actually do—and what each drill isolates. [Open the corpus](https://jerry-wisdom.vercel.app/drills/).
- **[Taste](https://github.com/jerryshi042003/taste)** — Remembers why a work mattered—not just the rating—then uses that reason to choose what deserves attention next. [Open Taste](https://jerry-taste.vercel.app/).
- **[David Choe archive](https://github.com/jerryshi042003/david-choe-transcript-archive)** — Search 434 transcripts across 475 hours of recorded conversations without scrubbing through a timeline. [Open the archive](https://wisdom-reader.onrender.com/choe-archive/).
