## Work animations · October 29, 2026

`shield-loading.gif` is cut from the Shield loading sequence as captured on October 29, 2026 (the production film's `loader.mp4`, a timestamped screencast of the live app at 1728×790, rendered here at 500px, 12 fps). It shows the particle galaxy, the quasar star and the LA Galaxy crest with the "Loading Shield" progress line; no report data appears. The particle logo engine is [Thinking Logos](https://github.com/enesozturk/thinking-logos) by Enes Ozturk, built on [Thinking Orbs](https://github.com/Jakubantalik/thinking-orbs) by Jakub Antalik, both MIT; the galaxy art, flight and crest bake are original.

`spom-cards.gif` was recorded on October 29, 2026 from the SPOM recognition studio (the report's own card component in a local gallery) with Playwright in Chrome at 1280×900, cropped to the card row. The five names are fictional and the amounts were rescaled; the clip is not production data. The LA Galaxy crest is the club's mark. Card motion uses Framer Motion and NumberFlow; the foil, flip faces and edge flames are original CSS and WebGL.

`shield-preview.jpg`, `cycling-preview.jpg`, `radio-preview.jpg` and `orfeu-preview.jpg` are the October 22 previews above re-cut to one 960×540 frame each: the Shield interface capture padded to 16:9, the Orfeu split poster resized, Cycling and Radio unchanged.

`orfeu-preview.jpg` and `radio-preview.jpg` were replaced on October 29, 2026 with the exact card thumbnails the portfolio home page uses: `hub/cache/sound-atlas-cumbia-71be148ab5d2.webp` (Orfeu) and `hub/cache/selected-radio-57373f94459c.jpg` (Radio), both at 960×540.

`shield-loading.gif` was re-cut on October 29, 2026 from the full intro capture (`full-intro.mp4`, 9.27 s) so it includes the crest flying into the header corner and the report fading in. From 8.75 s the report body below the header is blurred in the GIF; the production figures on that screen are not published.

Both GIFs were re-rendered on October 30, 2026 at higher quality: `shield-loading.gif` at 800px, 12 fps, 256 colors (24.9 MB); `spom-cards.gif` recorded at 2× pixel density and cut at 1000px, 15 fps, 256 colors (9.9 MB). The SPOM clip now ends after the card flips back; the simulated promotion was removed.

`usta-stadium.gif` (October 30, 2026) is a Playwright recording of the portfolio's USTA page at `/usta/?beat=data` from `shishi88` main at 4a88bfe9b (PRs 273 and 275), with the page header, caption, HUD and labels hidden by CSS; 15 s at 640px, 12 fps. The stadium, crowd and court are the page's own scene; the Shelton and Coria bodies are FlowHMR reconstructions from match footage with the serve counter-translated to stay behind the baseline; the ball flight is modelled. Arthur Ashe Stadium and US Open marks identify the venue.
